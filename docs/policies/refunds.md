# Refund Policy

## Automatic Full Refunds
The campus laundry service enforces a strict, transparent refund policy. All refunds in our system are **100% full refunds**. There are no partial refunds, no handling charges, and no time-decay deductions.

Refunds are triggered automatically in the following scenarios:

1. **Student Cancellation Before Slot Start**:
   If you cancel an upcoming booking before the start time, the system automatically triggers an immediate full refund to your original payment method.

2. **Machine Failed to Start (5-Minute Grace Period)**:
   If your paid booking starts but the physical machine fails to detect a running cycle (amps remain below operating threshold) within 5 minutes after the slot start time, the booking status automatically transitions to **Failed** (`failed`), and a full refund is processed instantly.

3. **Payment Captured Without Valid Booking**:
   If an unexpected network issue or latency causes a payment to be captured when the corresponding booking had expired, was cancelled, or was taken, the system detects the anomaly immediately and issues an automatic full refund.

4. **Machine Marked Out of Service**:
   If staff marks a machine out of service or a critical hardware error occurs prior to your scheduled cycle, all affected bookings are refunded in full.

## Refund Timing and Bank Processing
Once our backend issues a refund through Razorpay, our system records the transaction immediately. The actual appearance of funds in your bank account, credit card, or UPI wallet depends on your issuing bank:
- UPI and instant refunds typically reflect within 1 to 2 hours.
- Debit and credit card refunds normally take 3 to 5 business days, depending on your bank's settlement cycle.
Students can view the Razorpay refund tracking identifier directly in their Transaction History.
