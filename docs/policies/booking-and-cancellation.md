# Booking and Cancellation Policy

## Booking a Slot
Students can reserve an available laundry slot on any operational washer or dryer through the campus laundry web application. When you select an available slot and initiate checkout, the system places a temporary 5-minute hold on that slot (`pending_payment`).

During this 5-minute payment window:
- The slot is reserved exclusively for you.
- Other students will see the slot marked as **Reserved** and cannot book it.
- You must complete the payment via Razorpay before the 5-minute timer expires.
- If payment is completed successfully, the booking immediately updates to **Paid** (`paid`).
- If you do not complete payment before the 5 minutes elapse, the hold automatically expires (`expired`), releasing the slot back to the community or the next student on the waiting list.

## Slot Duration and Machine Operating Hours
Standard washing and drying slots are configured for 45 minutes by default. Please ensure you arrive promptly at the scheduled start time so your cycle runs without interfering with subsequent bookings.

## Cancellation and Immediate Full Refund
We understand that student schedules change. You may cancel any upcoming booking before its scheduled slot start time directly from your dashboard:
- **Full Refund**: Cancellations always receive a 100% full refund through Razorpay.
- **Zero Cancellation Fees**: There are never cancellation fees or penalties of any kind.
- **No Staff Review Required**: Refunds are issued automatically and immediately upon cancellation by the software.
- **Instant Slot Release**: As soon as you cancel, the slot is instantly freed up for other students or offered to the waiting list.

Once a slot start time has arrived and the machine begins operating, the booking is active and cannot be cancelled.
