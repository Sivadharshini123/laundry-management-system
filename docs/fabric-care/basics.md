# Fabric Care and Washing Basics

Proper fabric care protects your garments and prevents machine jams or lint buildup in campus machines.

## 1. Sorting Garments
Always separate clothes before loading into the washers:
- **Whites and Lights**: White shirts, light undershirts, socks, and bedsheets.
- **Darks and Colors**: Jeans, dark hoodies, black tees, and brightly dyed fabrics.
- **Delicates**: Synthetics, athletic gear, silks, and wool blends.
- **Towels and Heavy Linens**: Wash separately from lighter fabrics to prevent heavy lint transfer.

## 2. Water Temperatures
- **Cold Water (30°C / Eco)**: Recommended for darks, colors, fine knits, and delicate synthetic fabrics to prevent color fading and shrinkage.
- **Warm Water (40°C)**: Ideal for regular casual wear, light cottons, and moderately soiled items.
- **Hot Water (60°C)**: Recommended strictly for white cotton towels, bed linens, and sanitizing workout clothes.

## 3. Detergent Guidelines
- Use high-efficiency (HE) liquid detergent whenever possible.
- Do not exceed the recommended fill line on detergent caps; excess soap causes heavy suds, residue on clothing, and potential drainage errors.
- Never place detergent pods directly inside liquid dispenser drawers; place pods at the rear of the empty drum before adding clothes.

## 4. Drying Recommendations
- **Low Heat / Tumble**: Delicates, activewear, nylon, and synthetic apparel.
- **Medium Heat**: Permanent press, casual cottons, and blended fabrics.
- **High Heat**: Heavy cotton towels, jeans, and bedsheets.
- **Clean the Lint Trap**: Always check and clear the lint filter before and after each dryer cycle to maintain optimal airflow and prevent safety hazards.
